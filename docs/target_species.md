# Target species, round 2

Chosen from existing BirdNET results for the full 2021 season (63,670
recordings): species detected at confidence ≥ 0.8 in many recordings and at
most or all of a site's ARUs, excluding non-breeding species and likely
misidentifications. Northern Mockingbird is a target at every site by choice.

**Label all 20 species in every recording at every site**, so that each
recording is a valid "absent" example for species not heard in it.

## Notes

- *Passerina* (Blue Grosbeak, Painted Bunting, Indigo Bunting): all three
  occur at LWMA and have similar songs; review these labels carefully.
- *Tyrannus* (Scissor-tailed Flycatcher, Cassin's Kingbird): similar calls,
  but targets at different sites.
- Northern Mockingbird: BirdNET rarely detects it at CBMA and LWMA, likely
  because it credits mimicked song to the imitated species. Expect to add
  many labels by hand; Kiowa and SSWMA are the best sources of examples.
- Not chosen: Barred Owl (detections at grassland CBMA are almost certainly
  misidentifications), Upland Sandpiper (probably mostly migrant flight
  calls), Northern Cardinal (too few detections).
